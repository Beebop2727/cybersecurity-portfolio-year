# 04 — Healthcare cyber risk register

## Overview

A mock cyber risk register for a fictional NHS-style healthcare organisation. Covers realistic risks including phishing, ransomware, unsupported systems, weak MFA adoption, supplier risk, and backup failure — scored by likelihood and impact with existing controls and mitigation plans documented.

---

## Objectives

- Demonstrate understanding of cyber risk in a public-sector healthcare context
- Apply a structured risk management methodology
- Produce a professional risk register suitable for a governance audience
- Show awareness of NHS-specific threats and regulatory context

---

## Skills demonstrated

- Governance and risk management
- Healthcare and public-sector context
- Control mapping
- Professional documentation
